# 📊 B2B SaaS Customer Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer, marketing, and sales performance for a Business-to-Business (B2B) Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) company. The goal was to transform raw operational data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), SQL querying, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard enables business leaders to monitor revenue, evaluate marketing effectiveness, understand customer behavior, assess sales performance, and identify strategic opportunities for growth.

---

# Business Problem

The company collected large volumes of customer and marketing data but lacked a centralized reporting solution for monitoring business performance.

Decision-makers required a dashboard capable of answering critical business questions, including:

- Which marketing channels generate the highest return?
- Which subscription plans contribute the most revenue?
- Which sales representatives consistently outperform others?
- Which customers generate the greatest lifetime value?
- Where should marketing and retention efforts be focused?

# Key Business Findings

Some of the most significant insights discovered during the analysis include:

- Email marketing generated the highest revenue.
- Paid Search achieved the strongest ROAS.
- Referral customers recorded the highest average lifetime value.
- Customer acquisition costs varied significantly across marketing channels.
- Enterprise customers generated strong revenue despite a smaller customer base.
- Customer satisfaction remained consistently high across most segments.
- Revenue generation varied considerably across sales representatives.
- Nigeria contributed the highest customer volume and revenue.

---

# Data Quality Findings

During analysis, several data quality observations were identified:

- Missing values were present in fields such as Sales Representative, Marketing Channel, and Subscription Plan, resulting in "Blank" and "Unassigned" categories.
- Some sales representatives recorded zero revenue and zero lifetime value, indicating either inactive accounts or incomplete records.
- These categories were retained during analysis to highlight potential data quality issues rather than removing them.

---

# Strategic Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

- Increase investment in the highest-performing marketing channels.
- Replicate the strategies of top-performing sales representatives.
- Strengthen retention initiatives in high-churn regions.
- Expand high-value subscription plans.
- Improve data quality processes to reduce missing assignments and incomplete records.
- Continue monitoring customer satisfaction to maintain long-term growth.
