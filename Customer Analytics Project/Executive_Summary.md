**Date:** June 2026

# Project Goal

The purpose of this project was to analyze customer purchasing behavior, marketing effectiveness, and customer support trends in order to identify business improvement opportunities.

---

# Key Findings

## 1. Marketing Spend Is Not Optimized

The analysis revealed significant differences in marketing channel performance.

- Instagram achieved a lower completion rate compared to Organic Search
- Organic Search consistently produced stronger conversion performance

This indicates that marketing resources may not currently be allocated efficiently.

---

## 2. Delivery Issues Are Damaging Customer Experience

Late Delivery emerged as the most common support complaint.

Customers reporting delivery issues also recorded the lowest satisfaction scores, making logistics performance a major operational concern.

---

## 3. Revenue Is Highly Concentrated In Lagos

A large percentage of total revenue came from Lagos alone.

While this reflects strong market performance in the region, it also creates business risk due to overdependence on a single location.

---

# Recommendations

Improve delivery and logistics operations.
Outcome: Higher customer satisfaction and reduced complaints.

Reallocate part of the Instagram budget into Organic Search.
Outcome: Improved conversion performance.

Expand campaigns into additional cities.
Outcome: OutcomeReduced revenue concentration risk

Introduce VIP customer loyalty incentives.
Outcome: Improved retention and repeat purchases

---

# Final Conclusion

The business demonstrates strong revenue potential, but operational inefficiencies and uneven marketing performance are limiting growth opportunities.

Improving logistics performance, optimizing marketing allocation, and strengthening customer retention should be treated as immediate priorities.
